Participant Information

Language is fundamental to human life, shaping how we communicate and navigate the world. In everyday situations, however, our ability to process language can be challenged, for example when loud noises makes the understanding more difficult. This is exactly what we are exploring in this study: individual language understanding under difficult circumstances. Additionally, we want to find out whether our ability to understand language in noise is influenced by certain personality traits. Importantly, there are no right or wrong answers in this study – we are interested in your personal perception of language.

The entire experiment will take approximately 75 minutes.

What will I be doing?

This study consists of two parts, which will take about 75 min in total. In the first part, we ask you to complete 4 questionnaires, which will take about 25 minutes. In the second part, you will complete a language task, which will take about 50 minutes, and which is devided in 3 blocks between which you may take a short break (max. 5 min).

Please note that it is very important that you are in an undisturbed, quiet place and use headphones, since we will present you with auditory stimuli.


Is there a personal benefit from participating in the study?

You will not gain any personal benefit from participating. The results of the study aim to advance our understanding of important cognitive processes.


Are there risks associated with participating in the study?

Since this study is conducted online, the risks are minimal. However, computer-based tasks can be surprisingly tiring. During self-timed breaks, you will have the opportunity to stretch or rest your eyes. Once you start a questionnaire or the language task, we ask you to give it your full attention until you have completed that section.


Will there be any additional costs?

There are no costs for you to participate in the study.


Will I receive compensation for my time?

You will receive a fix pay of £11.25 for this study (75 min). Individuals who miss more than one attention check will be excluded.

What is expected of me?

We expect you to complete the study seated upright at a table or desk in a distraction-free environment (browser window maximized, and with no music, tabs, or other applications in the background). Please use headphones and switch on your microphone. Please silence your phone and place it out of sight for the experiment's duration. It is essential to perform all tasks in this study attentively and thoroughly.

We perform strict attention checks and rigorously exclude individuals from the study if attention checks are failed.


Can I withdraw from the study later?

Your participation is voluntary, and you can withdraw from the study at any time without providing a reason and without any penalties. Once the data has been fully anonymized (excluding your pseudonymized Prolific ID), it will no longer be traceable to you, and deletion will no longer be possible from that point.
